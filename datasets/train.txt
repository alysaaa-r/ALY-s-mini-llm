Topic: Vision
Description: Trinidad Municipal College (TMC) envisions itself as a model institution with fully developed academic, technical-vocational education and skill of manpower with positive work attitudes anchored in the core values of leadership and professionalism essential in the creation of self-reliant citizens.

Topic: Mission
Description: TMC's mission is to build well-trained, competent and employable professionals, who will meet the demands of the local and international work places.

Topic: Goal
Description: TMC aims at evolving a whole individual as a child of God and a member of a democratic society who is professionally competent that can provide leadership and advance knowledge, well-trained in a certain vocation not only to help himself but to help others, and practical yet responsible and obedient to the laws of God and to the laws of the government.

Topic: Philosophy
Description: TMC adheres to the philosophy that education is life and growth guided by faith in God and love of fellowmen in an environment of competitiveness, professionalism and excellence.

Topic: Slogan
Description: The official slogan and motto of the school is: "TMC is committed to public educational services second to none."

Topic: TMC History
Description: TMC's story began in 1980 when Mr. Paciano Petarco and Mayor Atty. Avelino N. Puracan converted the old 1957 Municipal Hall into a private vocational school called Trinidad Institute of Technology. In 1985, it was registered as Trinidad Junior College. On August 1, 2003, it was transformed into Trinidad Municipal College under the local government. It was officially recognized by CHED as a Higher Education Institution on August 18, 2020.

Topic: TMC Leadership and Enrollment
Description: TMC is currently led by Mayor Atty. Roberto C. Cajes, Ph.D. The institution has seen massive growth, with enrollment increasing from just 510 students in 2007 to more than 7,000 students by the 2022-2023 academic year.

Topic: TMC Courses and Programs
Description: TMC offers CHED-recognized programs including BS in Information Technology, BS in Office Administration, BA in Political Science, BA in Communication, Bachelor of Secondary Education (English), Bachelor of Elementary Education, and BS in Criminology. It also offers TESDA programs like Automotive Servicing NC-I and Shielded Metal Arc Welding NC-I and NC-II.

Topic: TMC Achievements
Description: A notable academic achievement for TMC occurred during the June 2022 Licensure Examination for Criminologists, where the school placed 8th nationwide in the Philippines through Criminologist Crisiolo M. Langamen Jr., a graduate of Batch 2021.

Topic: TMC Admission and Affordability
Description: TMC is the only LGU-managed institution in the area offering free higher education as a CHED-recognized Local University and College. It welcomes students from all walks of life regardless of socio-economic condition, disability, religion, race, or cultural background, provided they meet the admission requirements.
